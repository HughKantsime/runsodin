# O.D.I.N. — NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2: how O.D.I.N. supports your controls

## 1. Scope and disclaimer

This document maps O.D.I.N. 2.2.0 and later features to NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 requirements. Each feature below supports, or helps the customer meet, the named requirement. It is not an assessment result or an attestation, and TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md §3.4 states that no feature is a certification or authorization under any program.
The customer remains responsible for every control. O.D.I.N. is self-hosted, so infrastructure, network, physical and personnel controls are the customer's.
"Tier" comes from `backend/core/license_tiers.py`. A feature with no flag in that file is available on all tiers.
"ITAR mode" means `ODIN_ITAR_MODE=1`, a container-level environment variable (`backend/core/itar.py` docstring).
Each row is limited to the behaviour its evidence describes.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

- The customer is responsible for every control in this mapping. O.D.I.N. only supports it.
- Infrastructure, network, physical and personnel controls are the customer's, because O.D.I.N. is self-hosted.
- The customer provides TLS for inbound access, normally a reverse proxy (SECURITY.md "Hardening Recommendations").
- The customer secures the host, database and backups, including `.audit-hmac-keys.json` and the installation Ed25519 key.
- The customer chooses and secures SIEM receivers and decides whether to acknowledge each as a trusted boundary.
- The customer defines CUI/ITAR markings, audiences and retention, and handles downloaded exports and backups.
- The customer sets `ODIN_ITAR_MODE`, the IP allowlist and its own identity provider.
- The customer retains and reviews audit evidence. Independent signed exports or SIEM copies are advised (`docs/AUDIT_INTEGRITY.md`).
- The customer applies updates and screens personnel.

## 4. Not provided

- No FIPS-validated cryptography claim. The audit chain, Ed25519 signatures and bcrypt are used, with no validated module.
- No SAML. SSO is OIDC only, with one identity provider per instance (`oidc_config LIMIT 1`).
- No certification, authorization or attestation under any program (TERMS §3.4).
- No configurable idle-session timeout or session lock. The JWT lifetime is a fixed 24 hours (`backend/core/auth.py` `ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRE_HOURS`).
- No password reuse prevention.
- No media sanitization (3.8.3). Timelapse retirement deletes files; it is not sanitization of storage.
- No malware protection, vulnerability scanning, or physical and personnel controls.
- No encryption of CUI file content at rest. Fernet covers stored credentials and secrets only (SECURITY.md "Data Protection").

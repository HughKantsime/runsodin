CTEC POC

ODIN Education handoff

A self-hosted, bounded pilot for class/club cost centers, student-supplied sliced prints, teacher review, Google or Entra login, and optional read-only Google Classroom rostering.

Honest hardware boundary: begin with approved Bambu X1 Carbon/P1S printers and sliced .3mf. K1/Moonraker .gcode, H2S/H2C, and X-one² are not certified by this handoff.

CTEC deployment context

  • Approximately 354 students and 20 staff.
  • VM-hosted container with separate server, user, and printer VLANs.
  • Google Workspace preferred for OIDC; Entra remains supported.
  • Students slice in OrcaSlicer. Cloud slicing and Classroom coursework are out of scope.

VM and security prerequisites

  1. Dedicated Linux VM with Docker Engine/Compose, persistent storage, DNS/NTP, and capacity for two verified database copies plus pilot uploads.
  2. Stable school-trusted HTTPS hostname.
  3. TRUSTED_HOSTS set to the exact hostname, COOKIE_SECURE=true, COOKIE_SAMESITE=lax, and OIDC_REDIRECT_URI=https://HOST/api/auth/oidc/callback.
  4. Unique JWT_SECRET_KEY and ENCRYPTION_KEY transferred through an approved secret channel—not email or this report.
  5. Signed Education entitlement installed and Education mode enabled.

Google login

  1. Create a Google web OAuth client and authorize https://HOST/api/auth/oidc/callback.
  2. In Settings → Access → Authentication, select Google Workspace; enter the client ID/secret, Workspace domain, and ODIN tenant; keep openid profile email; enable SSO.
  3. Test one approved staff and one pilot student account. ODIN validates signature, audience, exact Google issuer, expiry, one-time nonce, verified email, and signed hosted domain.

Google login and Classroom consent are deliberately separate.

Optional Classroom roster

  1. Enable Google Classroom API and authorize https://HOST/api/education/classroom/callback.
  2. Education → Google Classroom: save the OAuth client/domain and authorize a tenant admin who can see the pilot course.
  3. Review the full teacher/student preview and diff, then explicitly import. Teachers become cost-center managers; students become cost-center students; new global roles remain viewer.
  4. Confirmed resync revokes removed memberships without deleting users, submissions, other classes, or printer entitlements.
ODIN requests course-read, roster-read, profile-email, and identity scopes only—no coursework, grades, roster writes, or domain-wide delegation.

Pilot acceptance

  1. Use one class/club, one teacher, a small approved student roster, and one or two Bambu X1/P1 printers.
  2. Student signs in, sees only authorized centers, uploads a sliced .3mf, and sees their submission.
  3. Teacher sees only managed-center work, rejects one test with a reason, then compatibility-checks and approves one to an entitled printer.
  4. Observe the lifecycle without claiming physical success until the device reports it.
  5. Create and verify a backup; restore to a disposable instance; confirm roster/mapping/submissions survive and secrets remain encrypted.

Review artifacts/ctec-poc-preflight/ctec-poc-preflight.html. It is read-only and emits state, not secret values.

Backup, privacy, and teardown

  • Follow the backup/restore runbook; retain cryptographic keys separately.
  • Classroom disconnect clears retained Google authorization but preserves imported operational records.
  • User erasure removes direct Classroom identity, revokes active grants, and clears matching connected Google tokens while preserving required tombstoned facts.
  • Use exact-ID sandbox reset/purge only on disposable sandbox data.

Known limitations

  • Live Google consent needs CTEC's client, hostname, and approved test account. Local tests do not impersonate that evidence.
  • No automatic Classroom sync, coursework, grades, or mutations.
  • No cloud slicing. Bambu sliced .3mf is the POC path.
  • Production activation, CTEC network access, real student data, and live printer commands remain separate ship-gate actions.
